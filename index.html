World-Class Training Data

ASTRA is trained on 4 internationally recognized scientific datasets — from PhysioNet to NASA's own GeneLab.

🏃
PAMAP2 Dataset
Physical Activity Monitoring with IMU sensors. 119,000+ samples across 18 activities from UCI Machine Learning Repository — the gold standard for activity recognition.
119K+ SAMPLES · UCI
💓
BIDMC PPG Dataset
Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center PPG and respiration signals from PhysioNet. Used to train our ExtraTrees heart rate estimator achieving MAE of 1.10 BPM.
PHYSIONET · BIDMC

WESAD Dataset
Wearable Stress and Affect Detection dataset from UCI — multi-modal physiological signals (EDA, BVP, Temp) from wrist and chest sensors for stress classification.
